1. Information We Collect

When you authorize AI Academy Publisher (operated by BAGAOPT AI Studio) via TikTok Login Kit, we receive only the OAuth 2.0 tokens and basic profile identifiers (such as open_id, display name, and avatar) necessary to publish localized educational videos to your TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

3. Data Storage & Security

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored securely in encrypted local/server configuration files and are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties. We do not collect passwords or private messages.
